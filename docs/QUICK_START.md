# Standalone quick start

Prefer pictures? The [illustrated tutorial](ILLUSTRATED_GUIDE.md) shows each action, button hold and resulting screen.

Crepuscular Homunculus runs on the FM-1 by itself. Play, edit, sequence and save
directly on the instrument.
Use a comfortable listening level.

## Play a sound

After the creator logo, press HOME to dismiss the bubbling intro. Turn PRESETS to
choose a factory sound and play the FM-1 keys. Ordinary HOME is the LIVE sound.
The four knobs shape the sound; EDIT opens the labeled sound controls.
For synthesized kits the knobs are BODY, WIRE, DECAY and BITE.

## Choose a track's sound

Tap SEQ. SELECT chooses DRUM, MELODY, ATMOS or BASS; ALGORITHM chooses that track's
A-D bank. Press HOME to open TRACK SOUND for the selected track, then turn PRESETS.
Press SEQ and EDIT to enter steps with that same captured sound. PRESETS in step
entry changes only the selected track's capture. HOME again from TRACK SOUND returns
to independent LIVE; changing LIVE does not replace existing track captures.

## Timing and patterns

On the SEQ overview, Knob 1 sets BPM, Knob 2 chooses 16/32/64 steps, Knob 3 sets
SWING 0-100 and Knob 4 sets Ghost Mask. In SEQ EDIT, Knob 3 selects the step instead.
Zero swing is straight. Each bank stores all 64 steps; shortening a pattern hides
later steps without erasing them. Up to four notes can be stored on one step.
PLAY starts or stops playback. See the full control map for recording and piano roll.

## Edit individual notes in the piano roll

1. Open **SEQ**, select a track with SELECT and its A–D bank with ALGORITHM.
2. Hold **SEL** for 700 ms, then release it: **ARRANGEMENT** opens.
3. Tap **SEL**: **STEP EDIT** opens.
4. Hold **SEL** for 700 ms, then release it: **PIANO ROLL** opens.

This STEP EDIT page is different from SEQ EDIT, which the EDIT button opens.
In the roll, SELECT moves the step cursor and ALGORITHM moves the pitch cursor.
Tap SEL to add a note; REC deletes the note at the cursor. Knob 1 edits its
length and Knob 2 its velocity. EDIT returns to STEP EDIT.
See the [piano-roll guide](PIANO_ROLL.md) for track/bank controls and limits.

## Save your work

Stop PLAY, release keys and pedals, let all tails finish, press physical SAVE and
wait for SAVED/the unsaved indicator to clear. SAVE stores the complete four-track
song, all sixteen banks, captured sounds and settings. Saving may wait while sound
is active. Native SAVE survives a normal power cycle; it is not a separate backup
against replacing firmware or overwriting its flash storage.

## Displays and reset warning

GLO then SEL opens CONTROL FEEL: Knob 1 selects LINEAR/ACCEL and Knob 2 selects
HOMUNCULUS or THORN RIFT. HOME returns. SAVE retains the choice.

Holding HOME opens a reset warning. HOME cancels, and it expires after ten seconds.
Reset requires releasing every button and then pressing physical SEL to confirm.
Do not confirm reset merely to navigate. A reset is a deliberate song-changing action.

Eight voices are shared across LIVE and tracks, with at most four FM voices.
Held notes and release tails share that budget; large chords can steal older voices.
The FX button bypasses corrosion/BITE, not every sound-shaping function.
